— About the Room

Down a staircase,
past the amber glow,
there is a room.

Ember & Rye is a subterranean cocktail and jazz bar occupying a late-Victorian cellar beneath Soho's West End. Named for the char of a wood fire and the bite of a good rye, it is a place for those who arrive after ten and stay until things blur beautifully.


The drinks are built with patience. The music is never recorded. The light has never been turned up higher than it is at this moment.

XIX

A Victorian cellar. A permanent state of almost midnight.

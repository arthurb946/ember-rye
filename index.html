Ember & Rye sits on the seventh floor of a Georgian townhouse in the heart of Soho — a rooftop bar conceived around one idea: that a cocktail tastes better with London spread below you.

Our menu draws from the British larder — smoked, charred, cured — interpreted through the lens of a modern bar programme. Every drink is built with the same intention as the view: nothing extraneous, nothing overlooked.
